# About Arcmelo

Making worlds. Sharing the journey.

Arcmelo is an independent game studio. This is where we share our games, code experiments, and ideas from the development process.

## Work & notes

- **Work**: From small prototypes to complete projects, exploring how ideas become interactive experiences.
- **Journal**: Notes on game design, programming, art, and sound.
- **Stay in touch**: Subscribe through RSS or Atom for future updates.

## About this site

Built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and [Firefly](https://github.com/CuteLeaf/Firefly), with a minimal deep-space visual style. Firefly is based on [Fuwari](https://github.com/saicaca/fuwari).

Thank you to the open-source creators, and thank you for visiting.
